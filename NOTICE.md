# Notice

This repository contains the official implementation of
"Leveraging Vision-Language Models as Weak Annotators in Active Learning."

The instance-wise supervision-level selection strategy is adapted from ISOAL:
https://github.com/matsuo-shinnosuke/ISOAL

The strategy is integrated here with VLM-generated weak annotation,
transition-matrix estimation, forward correction, and fine-grained recognition
experiments developed for this research project.
